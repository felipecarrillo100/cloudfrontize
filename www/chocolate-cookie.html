Double Chocolate Chip Cookies

Prep time: 15 mins | Bake time: 10 mins

The ultimate soft and chewy chocolate cookie, packed with extra large chocolate chunks and a hint of sea salt.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup softened butter
  • 3/4 cup brown sugar
  • 1/2 cup white sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 cup cocoa powder
  • 1 cup dark chocolate chips

Instructions

Preheat your oven to 180°C. Mix the dry ingredients, fold in the chocolate chips, and bake until the edges are set but the centers are still soft!
