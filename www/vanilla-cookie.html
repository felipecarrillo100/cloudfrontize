Old Fashioned Vanilla Sugar Cookies

⭐ 5.0 Rating | Quick & Easy

A simple, elegant vanilla cookie with a soft, melt-in-your-mouth texture and a buttery finish.

What You'll Need

  • 1 cup unsalted butter (room temp)
  • 1 1/2 cups powdered sugar
  • 1 large egg
  • 2 tsp high-quality pure vanilla extract
  • 2 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • Granulated sugar (for rolling)

Quick Steps

Cream butter and sugar until fluffy. Add egg and vanilla. Gradually mix in flour and salt. Chill dough for 30 minutes, roll into balls, and bake at 175°C for 8-10 minutes.
